# 3-Minute Live Demo Script (EN & PT-BR) — NASA Space Apps 2026

This guided demonstration walks visitors and judges through the discovery-first UX without requiring prior combustion expertise.

Public app: [Flame in Freefall](https://flame-in-freefall-kiriworks.streamlit.app/).

---

## 0:00 – 0:45 | Discovery Home & Bilingual Mode (`home`)
1. Open the app (**English (EN)** + **Guided Exploration** active by default) and point out immediate 10-second clarity:
   - Headline: **Explore the Science of Fire in Space** — plain-English explanation of orbital freefall vs. gravity.
   - Earth (`1g`) vs. Microgravity (`~0g`) quick contrast, optional beginner glossary, and verified archive totals (**12 NASA PSI Studies**, **40 Preserved NASA Files**, **117 Extracted Tables**, and **15,524 Searchable Passages** with the local semantic model or **11,582 Searchable Passages** in the cloud lexical configuration).
   - Three clear starting experiences: **Discover the Science →**, **Explore Fire Safety →**, and **Investigate NASA Data →**.
2. Demonstrate instant bilingual switching via the single `EN | PT-BR` selector in the top bar (**Português (PT-BR)** ↔ **English (EN)**) while NASA study IDs, DOIs, headers, and numbers remain unaltered.

## 0:45 – 1:35 | Guided Journey 1 — Experience A (`journey_1`)
1. Click **Discover the Science →** on the home page:
   - **Step 1 of 4**: Interactive physics comparison (flame shape, oxygen diffusion, cool flames, smoke aerosols). Click **Continue →**.
   - **Step 2 of 4**: Meet **FLEX (`PSI-69`)** with safety context, documented cover provenance notice, and authentic `researchers_guide.pdf` (p. 36) preview.
   - **Step 3 of 4**: Inspect live `PSI-69` Plotly visualizations (*Oxygen vs. Visible Flame Extinction Diameter* and `'Burning rate; mm'` with explicit unit caveat) paired with the inline **How to Read This Chart** guide and retrieved NASA evidence cards.
   - **Step 4 of 4**: Review grounded findings vs. strict limits, demonstrate **← Back / Continue →**, or jump to Data Explorer / Journey 2.

## 1:35 – 2:20 | Guided Journey 2 & Safe Comparison (`journey_2` → `compare`)
1. Launch **Explore Fire Safety →** from the home page:
   - **Step 2**: Switch interactively across **Prevention** (`BASS`, `BASS-II`, `Saffire I–III`), **Detection** (`SAME`, `SAME-R`), and **Suppression** (`FLEX`, `FLEX-2`).
   - **Step 3**: Inspect `PSI-25 · BASS-II` solid-fuel flame data, `PSI-102 · SAME` Final Report (p. 13) + aerosol table, and the cross-pillar methodological warning (`BASS-II` vs. `FLEX`).
   - **Step 4**: Click **Open Study Comparison Tool →** (`Compare`), then highlight the sticky **← Return to Guided Journey** button at top and explicit *Joint quantitative pooling NOT validated* guardrail.

## 2:20 – 3:00 | Experience C — Explorer, Evidence Assistant & Sources (`explorer` → `assistant`)
1. Click **Investigate NASA Data →** to open **Data Explorer** (`PSI-69`): note progressive disclosure (engineering `⚙` controls stay collapsed in Guided Exploration mode while Plotly charts + **How to Read This Chart** stay prominent).
2. Switch to **Scientific Assistant**:
   - Click Example Question 1 (**FLEX droplet O₂ conditions (18–25%)**) to watch it execute immediately without extra clicks, surfacing the **Plain-Language Guide to These Results** above cited NASA passages (`PSI-69`, page/row, **SHA-256**).
   - Mention **Experience level → Advanced Research** in the sidebar for full numerical filters, index diagnostics, and raw metadata when deeper investigation is needed.
