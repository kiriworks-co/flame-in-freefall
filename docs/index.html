Why Freefall Changes Fire: Three Safety Pillars

Prevention

BASS · BASS-II · Saffire I–III · PSI-62

Explores how solid spacecraft materials ignite and spread flames under microgravity ventilation without upward buoyancy.

Detection

SAME · SAME-R

Characterizes microgravity smoke aerosol particle-size distributions to calibrate orbital and deep-space fire detectors.

Suppression

FLEX · FLEX-2 · PSI-117 · PSI-159

Investigates spherical liquid droplet burning, cool-flame persistence, and radiative extinction oxygen limits.
